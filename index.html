ARTISAN PROFILE

Hana Getachew

Interior and textile designer, and creative director of Bolé Road Textiles.

Practice · Ethiopian handwoven textiles and interiors

Born in Addis Ababa, Hana Getachew draws on Ethiopia’s color, textiles, and graphic patterns. Through Bolé Road Textiles, she develops contemporary designs with artisan weaving partners in Ethiopia, bringing traditional textile references into home furnishings.

Email ENQU MADE about Hana Getachew
ARTISAN PROFILE

Mahlet Afework

Ethiopian fashion designer and lead designer of MAFI MAFI.

Practice · Contemporary apparel using Ethiopian textile traditions

Mahlet Afework, also known as Mafi, founded MAFI MAFI in Addis Ababa in 2011. Her designs bring traditional textile techniques into contemporary clothing. The brand works with Ethiopian cotton and handwoven fabrics, and its production supports local artisan and garment-making skills.

Email ENQU MADE about Mahlet Afework
ARTISAN PROFILE

Mekdes Mesfin

Founder and creative designer of Demii Design.

Practice · Ethiopian handwoven cotton apparel

Mekdes Mesfin founded Demii Design, an Ethiopian clothing design and manufacturing business. She turns handwoven Ethiopian cotton into contemporary garments, building on skills learned from her mother and her own fashion studies. Her work connects local weaving and clothing production with a modern wardrobe.
